## 2. Detailed Basis of Estimate (BOE)

### A. Direct Labor ($45,000.00)
* **Key Personnel:** Rahshaun Chambers (Lead Systems Architect & Principal Investigator)
* **Hours & Rate:** 320 direct labor hours across 12 calendar weeks (~26.7 hours per week) at an unburdened direct hourly rate of **$140.625 / hour**.
* **Labor Allocation across Work Plan Tasks:**
  * *Task 1 (Customer Discovery & Interface Baselines):* **120 Hours** ($16,875.00)  
    Conducting ≥30 structured DAF discovery interviews across PEO Cyber & Networks, AFRL Information Directorate, and Space Systems Command; synthesizing user constraints and drafting Deliverable D1.
  * *Task 2 (Automated CAVP Test Bench & Hardware Enclave Integration):* **120 Hours** ($16,875.00)  
    Developing automated NIST FIPS 203/204 Known Answer Test (KAT) test benches; benchmarking embedded TPM 2.0 and discrete enclaves (Titan M2 / Knox Vault); drafting Deliverable D2.
  * *Task 3 (Phase II Transition SOW & Final Reporting):* **80 Hours** ($11,250.00)  
    Formulating the Phase II demonstration Statement of Work; pursuing and documenting transition interest; compiling the Final Feasibility Report (incorporating SF 298 and DD Form 882).

### B. Materials, Hardware & Specialized Compute ($15,000.00)
Direct equipment, hardware modules, and cloud computing resources required specifically for laboratory validation:
* **Tactical Single-Board Computers ($4,500.00):** x86_64 and ARM64 single-board computers for edge avionics environment simulation and performance profiling.
* **Hardware Security Enclaves ($3,500.00):** Discrete TPM 2.0 evaluation modules, physical test boards, and cryptographic interface adapters.
* **Specialized Telemetry & Test Harness Compute ($4,000.00):** Dedicated multi-tenant cloud broker instances, isolated Kafka streaming clusters, and automated continuous-integration runners.
* **Network Injection & Emulation Hardware ($3,000.00):** High-speed packet injection interfaces and line-rate socket saturation testing equipment for EWMA LIF shock absorber stress testing.

### C. Other Direct Costs — Independent Cryptographic Review ($5,000.00)
* **Scope:** Engagement of an independent cryptographic consultant with formal NVLAP / FIPS 140-3 laboratory testing expertise.
* **Objective:** Conduct a pre-assessment code and architectural audit of the automated CAVP test bench, entropy verification routines, and memory zeroization invariants, establishing readiness for formal Phase II accreditation.

### D. Indirect Costs / General & Administrative ($10,000.00)
* **Rate Basis:** Calculated as a provisional indirect rate of **15.38%** on total direct costs ($65,000.00 direct base).
* **Pool Inclusions:** Statutory corporate filings (Pennsylvania annual compliance), SAM.gov registration maintenance, legal, accounting, cloud telecommunications, and government portal administration.
