# QuantumFlex: paid proof first, recurring service second

**Superseded by [QUANTUMFLEX_PAID_PROOF.md](QUANTUMFLEX_PAID_PROOF.md).** The newer plan tests payment for a QuantumFlex release-admission control directly. Keep this file only as a record of the earlier hypothesis.

Prepared September 26, 2026. Proposed commercial experiment, not a revenue forecast.

## Decision

Use QuantumFlex to deliver a narrow, paid technical outcome now. Use the resulting evidence and customer relationships to earn recurring business. Allocate 80% of available commercial effort to selling and delivering one infrastructure offer, and 20% to qualifying one commercial intake pilot. Do not develop two full businesses at once.

The first objective is one cleared customer payment within 14 days. The next is repeatable delivery at a positive margin. Large cash flow requires distribution and delivery capacity; the repository alone cannot establish demand.

Assumptions: one primary delivery operator, no established sales pipeline, no advertising budget, and existing development hardware. Recalculate capacity when team availability is known. Cap initial discretionary experiment spending at $250; this is a proposed limit, not permission to spend.

## What changes from the supplied proposal

1. Replace broad Linux/PQC/AI consulting with one fixed-scope offer and one follow-on. Buyers must understand the deliverable without understanding QuantumFlex internals.
2. Replace free shadow pilots and automatic subscription conversion with a paid diagnostic and a separately accepted continuation. Payment is stronger demand evidence than an LOI.
3. Replace industry leakage percentages with each customer's actual channel volumes, response times, completed jobs, collections, and direct job costs. The supplied statistics are not established facts for these prospects.
4. Match the product to its channel. A form webhook does not answer missed telephone calls. The current intake prototype produces local draft recommendations; it does not send alerts or dispatch technicians.
5. Treat latency and availability as measurements until a supported deployment exists. Do not sell 99.9% availability, guaranteed recovery, immutable storage, certification, or regulatory compliance based on prototype tests.
6. Remove generalized food-safety time limits from sales copy and automated triage. Equipment reports belong with the contractor and responsible facility staff; the tool should not decide whether food is safe.
7. Remove claims that local MSPs lack Linux or private-AI expertise. Public services pages do not reveal internal staffing, buying intent, or capability gaps.

## Offer A: QuantumFlex Infrastructure Evidence Sprint

**Buyer:** an MSP owner or technical lead with a specific Linux or container environment and an unresolved evidence, access, or recovery task.

**Pitch:** “In three business days after access is ready, we document one Linux host's access and container configuration, identify the highest-priority gaps, and deliver a reproducible evidence pack and one agreed remediation in staging.”

**Test price:** $1,500; $750 deposit after scope acceptance, $750 after delivery against agreed acceptance criteria. These are price hypotheses, not researched market averages. Payment settlement and procurement may take longer than the delivery window.

**Scope:** one host, up to three containers, one stakeholder, eight delivery hours maximum. No production penetration testing or emergency incident response. More hosts, application rewrites, and additional remediation require a new scope.

**Deliverables:** dated asset/configuration inventory with collection commands; observed findings separated from unknowns; three prioritized recommendations; one customer-selected staging remediation; before/after evidence and rollback instructions; a 30-minute handoff. Acceptance is receipt of the agreed artifacts and demonstration of the selected change, not absence of all security weaknesses.

**QuantumFlex contribution:** reuse its evidence handling, configuration review patterns, hash manifests, and tested components where appropriate. Do not install experimental cryptographic transport merely to include the brand. The current CBOM generator contains environment-specific constants, fallback values, and placeholders; its output needs per-client correction and schema validation before it can be a customer deliverable.

**Delivery readiness:** prepare a redacted sample evidence pack on an operator-owned environment before offering a firm start date. Verify the collector, chosen remediation, restore path, and actual time required. Current repository inspection is not proof that this commercial service has been delivered successfully.

**Follow-on:** quote a $3,000 implementation sprint only after the evidence review establishes a bounded task, maximum 16 delivery hours. Collect 50% at acceptance and the balance at completion. Offer $500/month evidence refresh only for a documented scope capped at two hours/month, business-hours support, and separately priced changes. No implied 24/7 response.

At an illustrative internal labor cost of $75/hour, the $1,500 sprint with eight hours and $100 direct expenses leaves $800 contribution before overhead, acquisition effort, fees, and taxes. If it takes 16 hours, contribution falls to $200. Track all hours, including sales, to judge whether this is worth repeating.

## Offer B: QuantumFlex Intake Workflow Diagnostic

**Buyer:** a commercial HVAC/refrigeration service manager with enough written requests to evaluate and a named person who owns follow-up.

**Test price:** $750 upfront for a 14-day diagnostic, capped at six delivery hours, one intake channel, and 100 historical or shadow records. Start with approved, minimized historical exports. The existing production dispatch process remains the source of operational decisions.

**Deliverables:** intake-to-response baseline, duplicate/missing-field report, human-reviewed draft triage demonstration, and a measured recommendation on whether live integration is worthwhile. This is not a live emergency response service and does not promise incremental revenue.

**Qualification:** at least 30 usable written requests in the evaluation window or an agreed historical sample, permission to use them, and a dispatcher available to label outcomes. Fewer records means inconclusive evidence, not a successful trial. If the actual problem is missed phone calls, stop this offer and scope a separate integration with their existing phone/answering provider.

**Live continuation gate:** authenticated ingestion, tenant separation, provider-specific retry/replay tests, monitored queues, tested restore, approved data retention, operator ownership, and verified notification delivery must exist before selling live response benefits. A server's HTTP 202 is not a delivered customer SMS or dispatcher notification. Verify actual form-provider authentication support instead of assuming the pasted signature headers exist.

**Potential continuation:** $500/month only after a separate scope is accepted and delivery cost is measured. Live integration/setup is separately quoted. No automatic conversion. At $500/month, two hours at $75 plus $75 direct costs leaves $275 before overhead and sales effort; eight hours leaves a loss of $175.

## 14-day execution test

- Days 1–2: create the sample evidence pack, rehearse delivery on owned infrastructure, and qualify 20 unique accounts, starting with warm relationships. Prepare a one-page scope and invoicing details. Verify capacity before accepting a start date.
- Days 3–5: operator conducts personalized outreach and discovery. Target five substantive conversations, two scoped proposals, and one deposit. These are operating targets, not predicted conversion rates.
- Days 6–10: deliver the first paid sprint within eight hours. Record costs and acceptance; ask for permission to use a redacted result and for one introduction. Run the intake diagnostic only if paid and within capacity.
- Days 11–14: collect any due balance, offer a justified follow-on, and review the funnel and margin. Two infrastructure sprints plus one diagnostic would consume 22 delivery hours, excluding sales and sample preparation.

**Decision rules:** fewer than three conversations after 20 relevant contacts means revisit the list/channel/message. Five qualified conversations without a paid commitment means revisit urgency, trust, scope, or price before building more software. Two successful deliveries within the time cap support repeating the offer. An intake pilot with insufficient data remains inconclusive. Pause live intake rollout on lost records or unsafe advice until the fault is corrected.
