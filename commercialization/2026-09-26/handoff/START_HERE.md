## Decisions when a step fails

- **Demo error:** save the output, repair that failure, rerun the affected cases. Do not describe a failed run as passing. Customer readiness remains pending until their environment passes.
- **Wrong prospect:** disqualify immediately if they only need container/Java/Windows controls or already solve this exact problem. Do not expand the product to win one unsuitable pilot.
- **No reply after four business days:** send the single follow-up if authorized. Three nonresponses say very little about demand. At seven business days, close those attempts and prepare seven more evidence-matched prospects; get authorization for the new recipients.
- **Ten delivered contacts, fewer than two substantive replies after follow-up:** revise the message/channel once using the observed objections and prepare a second batch of ten. Do not build more features to solve an unconfirmed sales problem.
- **Three qualified conversations, no paid scope:** ask what prevented purchase and record the exact reason. Change one variable—scope, price, or buyer segment—and test it. No unsupported revenue forecasts.
- **Twenty appropriate delivered contacts and no qualified buyer:** mark the offer/channel unvalidated and hold an explicit continue/change/stop decision. This is a bounded experiment, not permission for indefinite effort or spend.
- **Paid technical failure:** stop rollout, deliver the failure evidence, agree a capped repair window or refund for undelivered scope. Revenue cannot turn a failed control into a pass.
