# Athena Knowledge Domain 01: Physics & Quantum Dynamics

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs Athena's cognitive reasoning in classical mechanics, thermodynamics, electrodynamics, special and general relativity, quantum mechanics, and quantum field theory.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Classical & Analytical Mechanics**: Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, phase space evolution, conservation laws via Noether's theorem, dynamical systems, chaos theory, and symplectic integrators.
2. **Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics**: Microcanonical, canonical, and grand canonical ensembles; entropy, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, fluctuation theorems, Landauer's principle minimum energy dissipation, and dissipative structures.
3. **Electrodynamics & Optics**: Maxwell's equations, gauge transformations, relativistic electrodynamics, wave propagation, cavity quantum electrodynamics, and metamaterials.
4. **Quantum Foundations**: Schrödinger equation, Hilbert spaces, density matrix formalisms, Heisenberg uncertainty principle, quantum measurement problem, open quantum systems, and Lindblad master equation.
5. **Relativity & Gravitation**: Differential geometry of spacetime, Einstein field equations, geodesic motion, gravitational wave propagation, and black hole thermodynamics.

## Scientific Mission
Athena formulates hypotheses with strict dimensional analysis, derives mathematical proofs step-by-step, respects conservation invariants, and distinguishes speculative theories from experimentally verified phenomena.
