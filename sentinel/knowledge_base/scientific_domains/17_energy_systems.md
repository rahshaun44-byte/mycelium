# Athena Knowledge Domain 17: Energy Systems & Clean Power Physics

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs Athena's cognitive capabilities across nuclear physics, magnetic and inertial confinement fusion, high-density electrochemical energy storage, smart grid load balancing, and thermodynamic power conversion.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Nuclear Fission & Advanced SMRs**: Neutron transport theory, Doppler broadening, point kinetics equations, molten salt and sodium-cooled fast reactors, passive safety systems, and closed fuel cycle physics.
2. **Controlled Fusion Dynamics**: Lawson criterion, magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) equilibrium (Grad-Shafranov equation), tokamak and stellarator magnetic confinement, gyrokinetic turbulence, and laser-driven inertial confinement.
3. **Advanced Electrochemical Storage**: Solid-state electrolyte ion transport kinetics, lithium-metal dendrite suppression, NMC/LFP cathode degradation mechanisms, redox flow batteries, and supercapacitive double-layer dynamics.
4. **Grid Stability & Microgrid Orchestration**: Frequency regulation (droop control), synthetic inertia, wide-area monitoring via synchrophasors (PMUs), AC/DC converter control, and dynamic line rating algorithms.
5. **Thermodynamic Conversion & Heat Cycles**: Supercritical CO2 Brayton cycles, organic Rankine cycles (ORC), thermoelectrics, and Carnot limit efficiency optimization under variable industrial heat rejection.

## Scientific Mission
Athena applies strict thermodynamic conservation laws ($\Delta U = Q - W$, second law entropy generation $S_{gen} \ge 0$), derives cycle efficiencies from first principles, models battery lifecycle degradation mathematically, and rejects unverified perpetual or over-unity energy claims.
