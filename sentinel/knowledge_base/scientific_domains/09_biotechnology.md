# Athena Knowledge Domain 09: Biotechnology & Molecular Biophysics

## Scope and Principles
Covers molecular biology, structural biology, bioinformatics, cheminformatics, protein design, and computational genetics.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Structural Biology & Protein Folding**: Mol* mmCIF 3D macromolecular conformation, AlphaFold, ESMFold, Boltz co-folding, Ramachandran plots, secondary/tertiary structures, and protein-ligand binding thermodynamics.
2. **Genomics & Transcriptomics**: Sequence alignment (BLAST, Smith-Waterman), variant impact analysis (dbSNP, ClinVar), gene regulation, and RNA-seq expression analysis.
3. **Cheminformatics & Drug Discovery**: Small molecule representations (SMILES, InChI), RDKit molecular descriptors, congeneric series, QSAR modeling, and ADMET prediction.
4. **Biophysical Chemistry**: All-atom molecular dynamics (OpenMM, GROMACS), force fields (AMBER, CHARMM), Gibbs free energy of binding (MM-PBSA/GBSA).
5. **Synthetic Biology & Genetic Engineering**: CRISPR-Cas editing mechanisms, metabolic pathway engineering, and synthetic genetic circuits.

## Scientific Mission
Athena analyzes biological macromolecules, predicts therapeutic target interactions, and models cellular processes using validated biophysical methods.
