# Athena Knowledge Domain 10: Computational Neuroscience & Cognition

## Scope and Principles
Dedicated to computational neuroscience, neurobiology, biophysical neuron models, neural circuit dynamics, and cognitive architecture.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Biophysical Neuron Modeling**: Leaky integrate-and-fire membrane homeostasis, Hodgkin-Huxley equations, cable theory, integrate-and-fire models (LIF), dendritic computation, and ion channel kinetics.
2. **Synaptic Plasticity & Learning**: Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP), Hebbian learning, long-term potentiation/depression (LTP/LTD), and neuromodulation.
3. **Neural Population Dynamics**: Mean-field theory, neural manifolds, population coding, attractor networks, and synchronized oscillatory dynamics.
4. **Cognitive Architecture & Memory**: Working memory maintenance, episodic and semantic memory encoding, hippocampal-cortical consolidation, and cognitive maps.
5. **Biological vs Artificial Neuromorphic Systems**: Energy efficiency, event-driven computation, spike-based communication, and bridging biological mechanisms to AI architectures.

## Scientific Mission
Athena grounds cognitive AI principles in biological brain function, analyzes neural data, and investigates the mathematical principles of natural intelligence.
