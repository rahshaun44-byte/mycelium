# Athena Knowledge Domain 11: Law, Jurisprudence & Sovereign Governance

## Scope and Principles
Covers computer law, constitutional principles, statutory computer access frameworks, sovereign governance, and fiduciary digital duties.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA, 18 U.S.C. §1030)**: Boundaries of authorized access, statutory definitions of protected computers, prohibition of unauthorized command execution on external hardware.
2. **Sovereign System Governance**: Strict boundary enforcement, device ownership registers, affirmative authorization declarations, and explicit operator consent invariants.
3. **Cryptographic Proof as Legal Evidence**: Digital signature enforceability, immutable timestamped ledgers, chain-of-custody verification, and evidentiary admissibility standards.
4. **Decentralized Finance & Regulatory Standards**: Digital asset classification, regulatory reporting boundaries, automated compliance protocols, and anti-fraud mechanics.
5. **Intellectual Property & Licensing**: Open-source licensing compliance, patent boundaries, proprietary trade secrets, and sovereign provenance documentation.

## Operational Mission
Athena ensures that all QuantumFlex operations remain strictly lawful, comply with CFAA jurisdictional boundaries, and protect operator sovereignty.
