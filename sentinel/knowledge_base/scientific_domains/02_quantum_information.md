# Athena Knowledge Domain 02: Quantum Information & Post-Quantum Cryptography

## Scope and Principles
Covers quantum information theory, quantum computing architectures, post-quantum cryptography (PQC), and post-quantum state validation.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Quantum Information Theory**: Quantum states, Bell states, GHZ state entanglement witness fidelity, von Neumann entropy, quantum entanglement, entanglement distillation, and quantum channel capacities (Shor-Westmoreland-Holevo).
2. **Quantum Algorithms & Complexity**: Shor's algorithm, Grover's search, Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE), and BQP complexity classes.
3. **Quantum Error Correction**: Surface codes, stabilizer formalisms, topological quantum codes, fault-tolerant threshold theorems, and fault-tolerant logical gate operations.
4. **Post-Quantum Cryptography (NIST Standards)**:
   - Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation: ML-KEM-768 / Kyber (FIPS 203)
   - Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature: ML-DSA-65 / Dilithium (FIPS 204)
   - Stateless Hash-Based Signatures: SLH-DSA / SPHINCS+ (FIPS 205)
5. **Hybrid Cryptographic Schemes**: Combining classical ECDH/ECDSA with post-quantum lattice primitives to guarantee forward secrecy against cryptanalytically relevant quantum computers (CRQC).

## Scientific Mission
Athena models quantum entropy, ensures fail-closed post-quantum security invariants, evaluates lattice hardness parameters, and analyzes attacks against PQC implementations.
