# Athena Knowledge Domain 15: Quantum Computing & PQC Milestones

## Scope and Principles
Monitors experimental and theoretical milestones in quantum hardware, quantum algorithms, and post-quantum migration mandates.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Quantum Hardware Progress**: Superconducting transmon qubits, trapped-ion systems, neutral atom arrays, photonic quantum computing, and silicon spin qubits.
2. **Quantum Supremacy & Error Correction**: Realization of logical qubits with distance-d surface codes, physical error reduction, and fault-tolerant milestone tracking.
3. **NIST Post-Quantum Migration**: Federal mandates (CNSA 2.0), deployment timelines for ML-KEM and ML-DSA, hybrid protocol rollouts in TLS 1.3 and SSH.
4. **Quantum Cryptanalysis Updates**: Progress on Shor's algorithm scaling requirements, lattice cryptanalysis algorithms (BKZ 2.0), and quantum key distribution (QKD).
5. **Quantum Sensing & Metrology**: Nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center diamond sensors, atom interferometers, and ultra-high-precision quantum measurements.

## Operational Mission
Athena monitors the cryptographic transition horizon, validates post-quantum resistance, and tracks empirical quantum computing advancements.
