# Athena Knowledge Domain 21: International Law, Maritime & Space Jurisdictions

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs jurisdictional sovereignty, public international law, maritime legal regimes (UNCLOS), outer space treaty governance (OST 1967), international dispute resolution, and cross-border digital jurisdiction.

## Dual-Score Evidence Classification
- **Intellectual / Scientific Importance ($S_I$)**: 0.90 / 1.00
- **Empirical Support ($E_S$)**: 0.95 / 1.00
- **Epistemic Classification**: Codified International Treaties & Customary Law ($E_S \ge 0.95$)

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Sovereignty & State Jurisdiction**: Westphalian sovereignty principles, territoriality principle, nationality principle, protective principle, passive personality, and universal jurisdiction under customary international law.
2. **Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)**: Internal waters, territorial seas (12 nautical miles), contiguous zones (24 nm), Exclusive Economic Zones (EEZ, 200 nm), high seas freedoms, and sovereign immunity of non-commercial state vessels.
3. **Outer Space Treaty (OST 1967) & Space Law**: Non-appropriation principle (Article II), state responsibility for national activities in space (Article VI), registration of space objects, liability for damages (Liability Convention 1972), and planetary protection.
4. **Cross-Border Digital Jurisdictions & Conflict of Laws**: Extraterritorial application of data protection frameworks (GDPR), CLOUD Act extraterritoriality, sovereign cloud mandates, and jurisdictional challenges in distributed ledger nodes.
5. **International Arbitration & Enforceability**: New York Convention of 1958 (recognition and enforcement of foreign arbitral awards), ICSID investor-state dispute settlement, and choice-of-law clause enforceability in algorithmic smart contracts.

## Operational Mission
Athena ensures that QuantumFlex multi-node operations respect international law boundaries, identify jurisdictional nexus points cleanly, and maintain cryptographic compliance standards defensible before international arbitral tribunals.
