# Athena Knowledge Domain 25: Environmental Science & Climate Informatics

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs planetary geophysical dynamics, climate modeling, atmospheric thermodynamics, remote sensing telemetry, biogeochemical carbon cycles, and geospatial climate informatics.

## Dual-Score Evidence Classification
- **Intellectual / Scientific Importance ($S_I$)**: 0.90 / 1.00
- **Empirical Support ($E_S$)**: 0.93 / 1.00
- **Epistemic Classification**: Geophysical Fluid Dynamics & Multi-Decadal Sensor Data ($0.80 \le E_S < 0.95$)

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Geophysical Fluid Dynamics & Atmospheric Modeling**: Navier-Stokes equations on a rotating sphere (Coriolis acceleration, geostrophic balance), baroclinic instability, Rossby waves, primitive equations, and general circulation models (GCMs).
2. **Radiation Transfer & Thermodynamic Balance**: Stefan-Boltzmann radiative equilibrium, greenhouse gas spectral absorption bands (HITRAN database), climate sensitivity metrics, radiative forcing calculations ($\text{W/m}^2$), and aerosol-cloud interactions.
3. **Satellite Remote Sensing & Earth Observation**: Multispectral and hyperspectral imaging, synthetic aperture radar (SAR) interferometry (InSAR), normalized difference vegetation index (NDVI), land surface temperature (LST), and LiDAR canopy profiling.
4. **Biogeochemical & Carbon Cycle Dynamics**: Oceanic thermohaline circulation, carbon sequestration flux rates, terrestrial carbon sinks, methane clathrate feedback loops, and ocean acidification carbonate chemistry.
5. **Climate Informatics & Extreme Event Statistics**: Spatial-temporal graph neural networks, extreme value theory (Generalized Pareto and GEV distributions), hydrological flood forecasting, and multi-sensor environmental telemetry fusion.

## Scientific Mission
Athena models earth system dynamics with strict conservation of mass, energy, and angular momentum, analyzes satellite telemetry with rigorous radiometric calibration, and quantifies climate prediction uncertainties using formal ensemble statistics.
