# Athena Knowledge Domain 18: Materials Science & Condensed Matter Physics

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs Athena's cognition in solid-state physics, electronic band structure, crystalline defects, topological matter, high-temperature superconductivity, and nanostructured materials.

## Dual-Score Evidence Classification
- **Intellectual / Scientific Importance ($S_I$)**: 0.96 / 1.00
- **Empirical Support ($E_S$)**: 0.89 / 1.00

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Electronic Band Structure & Quantum Transport**: Tight-binding approximations, density functional theory (DFT), Boltzmann transport equation, quasiparticle excitations, and electron-phonon coupling mechanisms.
2. **Superconductivity & Unconventional Pairing**: BCS microscopic theory, Ginzburg-Landau phenomenology, cuprate and pnictide phase diagrams, nickelate heterostructures, and Andreev reflection.
3. **Topological Insulators & Semimetals**: Berry phase, Chern numbers, quantum spin Hall effect, Weyl and Dirac semimetals, and Majorana zero modes in topological superconductor nanowires.
4. **Semiconductor & 2D Nanostructures**: Transition metal dichalcogenides (TMDs), graphene moiré superlattices (magic-angle twistronics), wide-bandgap semiconductors (GaN, SiC, diamond), and quantum dot excitonic confinement.
5. **Crystallography & Defect Thermodynamics**: Point defects (Kröger-Vink notation), dislocation dynamics, grain boundary kinetics, amorphous phase transitions, and X-ray diffraction (XRD) Rietveld refinement.

## Scientific Mission
Athena integrates quantum mechanical principles with crystallographic symmetry groups, models electronic dispersions rigorously, computes material strain tensors analytically, and validates theoretical band gaps against spectroscopic evidence.
