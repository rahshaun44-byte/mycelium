# Athena Knowledge Domain 19: Aerospace, Astrodynamics & Space Systems

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs orbital mechanics, trajectory optimization, radiation-hardened avionic systems, propulsion thermodynamics, and satellite constellation telemetry.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Astrodynamics & Orbital Mechanics**: Two-body Keplerian orbits, patched conic approximations, restricted circular three-body problem (CR3BP), Lagrange equilibrium points ($L_1$ to $L_5$), and low-thrust continuous trajectory optimization.
2. **Propulsion Thermodynamics & Chemical Kinetics**: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation, isentropic nozzle expansion (De Laval nozzles), characteristic velocity ($c^*$), specific impulse ($I_{sp}$), Hall-effect thrusters, and pulsed plasma propulsion.
3. **Spacecraft Guidance, Navigation & Control (GNC)**: Attitude determination and control systems (ADCS), reaction wheel desaturation, quaternion kinematics, Kalman filtering for celestial navigation, and star tracker processing.
4. **Radiation Hardening & Orbital Environment**: Single-event effects (SEE, SEU, SEL), total ionizing dose (TID), Van Allen radiation belts, galactic cosmic rays, solar particle events, and triple-modular redundancy (TMR) FPGA design.
5. **Constellation Mesh & Inter-Satellite Links (ISL)**: Laser cross-links, Doppler shift compensation, Delay-Tolerant Networking (DTN / Bundle Protocol RFC 5050), and ground station handover coordination.

## Scientific Mission
Athena calculates orbital state vectors with relativistic and J2 harmonic perturbations, derives delta-v budgets strictly from conservation of momentum, and enforces thermal balance limits in vacuum environments.
