# Athena Knowledge Domain 14: Biotechnology & Life Sciences Frontiers

## Scope and Principles
Monitors breakthroughs at the intersection of computational biology, artificial intelligence, genetics, and biotechnology therapeutics.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Structural Biology Milestones**: AlphaFold developments, Boltz releases, cryo-EM computational reconstruction advances, and molecular design benchmarks.
2. **Genomic Medicine & Base Editing**: Prime editing, CRISPR therapeutic trials, non-viral vector delivery innovations, and personalized genomic therapies.
3. **AI-Driven Drug Discovery**: De novo small-molecule generation, target tractability assays, clinical trial outcome predictions, and chemical congeneric series curation.
4. **Neurotechnology & Brain-Computer Interfaces**: High-density neural probes, non-invasive EEG/fNIRS decoders, closed-loop neuromodulation, and neural prosthetics.
5. **Regulatory & Clinical Trial Approvals**: FDA guidance on AI-enabled medical devices, clinical pipeline progressions, and safety monitoring telemetry.

## Operational Mission
Athena synthesizes life sciences breakthroughs, validates biological claims against peer-reviewed literature, and maintains high-fidelity domain expertise.
