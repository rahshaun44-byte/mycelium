# Athena Knowledge Domain 23: Financial Markets & Automated Liquidity Engineering

## Scope and Principles
This domain governs high-frequency market microstructure, limit order book dynamics, stochastic volatility modeling, cross-asset liquidity risk, and algorithmic execution architectures.

## Dual-Score Evidence Classification
- **Intellectual / Scientific Importance ($S_I$)**: 0.91 / 1.00
- **Empirical Support ($E_S$)**: 0.94 / 1.00
- **Epistemic Classification**: Quantitative Mathematical Models & Empirical Tick Microstructure ($0.80 \le E_S < 0.95$)

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Limit Order Book (LOB) Dynamics**: Level 2 and Level 3 market data, order book shape and depth, order flow toxicity (VPIN metric), queue position estimation, market impact functions (Almgren-Chriss framework), and adverse selection.
2. **Stochastic Volatility & Derivative Pricing**: Heston stochastic volatility model, Black-Scholes-Merton PDE, Jump-diffusion models (Merton, Kou), implied volatility surface smile and smirk dynamics, and local volatility Dupire formulas.
3. **Automated Liquidity Provisioning**: Avellaneda-Stoikov market making model, inventory risk penalty ($\gamma$), optimal bid-ask spread determination, jump risk hedging, and zero-inventory day-close mandates.
4. **Execution Algorithms & Transaction Cost Analysis (TCA)**: Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP), Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP), Percentage of Volume (POV), implementation shortfall minimization, and dark pool execution logic.
5. **Systemic Risk & Liquidity Stress Testing**: Value at Risk (VaR), Conditional VaR (Expected Shortfall), Basel III/IV liquidity coverage ratios (LCR), clearinghouse margin mechanics, and flash crash cascade propagation modeling.

## Scientific Mission
Athena applies quantitative finance with rigorous risk boundaries, models market impact analytically, strictly prohibits unhedged runaway risk exposure, and ensures compliance with institutional regulatory reporting mandates.
