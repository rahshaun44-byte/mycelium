# Athena Knowledge Domain 04: Computational Science & Numerical Simulation

## Scope and Principles
Focuses on numerical methods, high-performance computing (HPC), physical simulations, error analysis, and scientific computing frameworks.

## Core Focus Areas
1. **Numerical Linear Algebra**: Direct and iterative solvers (GMRES, Conjugate Gradient), singular value decomposition (SVD), QR factorizations, and preconditioners.
2. **Differential Equation Solvers**: Runge-Kutta methods, symplectic integrators, finite difference methods (FDM), finite element analysis (FEA), and boundary element methods (BEM).
3. **High-Performance Architecture**: SIMD/AVX-512 optimization, GPU acceleration (CUDA, ROCm, OpenCL), MPI distributed memory computing, and OpenMP multi-threading.
4. **Monte Carlo & Stochastic Simulation**: Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), Metropolis-Hastings, Gibbs sampling, and Gillespie stochastic chemical kinetics.
5. **Formal Verification & Numerical Stability**: Formal verification F* theorem proving, reproducible builds, floating-point arithmetic error accumulation (IEEE 754), condition numbers, sensitivity analysis, and verification of scientific codes.

## Scientific Mission
Athena designs computationally efficient, stable numerical simulations, verifies convergence properties, and benchmarks empirical algorithms against analytical solutions.
