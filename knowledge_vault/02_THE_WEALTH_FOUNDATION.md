# 💰 The Wealth Foundation: Capital, Reserves, and The R >= O Protocol

## 1. The Core Truth About Money
Money is not status; money is **stored leverage and defense**.
* If you do not have money, your time belongs to someone else, and the people who depend on you are exposed to outside shocks.
* The goal of wealth is not consumption or luxury—it is **insulation, freedom, and sovereignty**.

## 2. The 3 Buckets of Capital
To build substantial wealth without gambling your family's security, capital must be partitioned into three non-negotiable buckets:

### Bucket 1: The Iron Reserve (The "R" in R >= O)
* **What it is:** 6 to 12 months of non-negotiable living expenses (rent/mortgage, food, healthcare, utilities).
* **Where it lives:** Completely liquid, unencumbered cash or short-term treasury bills in a secure bank.
* **The Rule:** This bucket is sacred. It is never invested in crypto, stocks, or high-risk business ventures. It is the fortress that protects your dependents.

### Bucket 2: The Cash-Flow Engine (The Productive Capital)
* **What it is:** Capital used to buy tools, compute, inventory, or infrastructure that directly produces cash flow (e.g., local AI appliances, client services, business tools).
* **The Rule:** Every dollar deployed here must have a clear, mathematical path to returning more than it cost.

### Bucket 3: The Asymmetric Research Fund (The Freedom Capital)
* **What it is:** Surplus profit produced by Bucket 2.
* **What it does:** Funds your deep research, speculative investments, patents, or new ventures.
* **The Rule:** If an asymmetric bet drops to zero, Buckets 1 and 2 remain 100% unharmed.

## 3. The Law of Negative Leverage (Obligations)
* Every debt, subscription, and recurring bill is an **Obligation (O)** that eats your freedom.
* When obligations drop, your break-even cost drops to near zero. You can think clearly, study deeply, and negotiate from a position of absolute power.
